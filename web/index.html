THE PROJECT

ClearTalk, explained.

A testable workspace for understanding speech when several people talk, in turn or at once.

01

What happens to a recording?

SepFormer estimates two speech sources from the mixture. A Whisper speech recognizer then turns the mixture and each source into text: either the pretrained model or ClearTalk's fine-tuned one. You can listen to every result and compare the text.

02

What is our own model?

Whisper small.en adapted with LoRA (parameter-efficient fine-tuning) to the kind of audio it receives here: voices extracted by the separator from noisy two-speaker mixtures, with their artefacts and 8 kHz bandwidth. Only small low-rank adapter matrices were trained, then merged, so it runs exactly as fast as the original.

03

How do we evaluate it?

We measure word error rate (substitutions + deletions + insertions over reference words) on complete held-out utterances with official transcripts, from speakers never used in training. Checkpoints are chosen on validation data; the test set is scored once. Speech sources come from partitions of LibriSpeech ↗, a corpus of read English audiobook speech. These are controlled mixtures, not natural conversations; their results do not establish real-world accuracy. Cohort sizes and available measurements are shown in the active model's evidence panel.

04

What does Conversation mode do?

It finds the stretches with speech, groups them by voice (speaker embeddings and clustering) so each turn gets a speaker label, detects the language of every turn among Kazakh, Russian and English, and sends the turn to a Whisper recognizer for that language. Ready recognizers are used as published; the project's own multilingual models, when installed, are Whisper models adapted with LoRA to the three languages and to recordings that change language. Which recognizer handles which language is decided by word error rate on validation data. If people switch language in the middle of a turn, choose "Languages change inside turns": every turn then goes to the recognizer trained for that. You can name the speakers, correct the text, export subtitles, and keep a corrected recording on this computer as a check example for measuring the recognizers on real speech.

05

What are the limits?

The two-voice modes take short English audio and separation assumes two speakers; A/B are clip-local outputs, not identified people. Conversation mode separates people who talk at the same time only when "2 · at the same time" is chosen for a short clip (a demonstration, not measured on a test set), speaker labels are not identities, and a change of language in the middle of a turn can be missed. Mixed-language quality was measured on recordings assembled from single-language utterances, not on natural language switching. Video and lip reading are future research, not active features. Listen back: transcripts may miss or invent words.

Recording privacy & retention

Results are private to this browser session and expire after 24 hours. The server owner can access stored recordings. Only upload audio you have permission to process.
